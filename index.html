The problem

Why expensive studio rooms sit unused.

Buying good equipment is easy. Making the room repeatable for a non-technical team is the hard part.

The room is not the product. Repeatable publishing is.

Too many steps

Cameras, lights, audio, cards, settings and cables must be rebuilt before every session.

02 · QUALITY

Inconsistent output

Sound changes, colors drift, files go missing and every recording feels different.

03 · OWNERSHIP

No clear operator

The team waits for the one technical person who knows how the room works.

04 · HANDOFF

Broken post-production

Editors receive unlabeled media, missing angles or recordings without reliable backup.

FAQ

Questions before you build.

Straight answers before equipment is ordered.

How long does a studio build take?
Typical delivery is 4–8 weeks after the room is ready, scope is signed and equipment is available. Construction dependencies or custom millwork can extend the schedule.
Do you handle construction and permits?
Equipment integration, low-voltage scope and acoustic treatment are defined directly. Electrical, structural, permitting and licensed-trade work are quoted through qualified partners where required.
Can a non-technical team operate the room?
That is the design goal. The system is built around presets, labeled controls, a written SOP, a real training session and post-install support.
Do you provide editing and content repurposing?
Editing and repurposing may be added separately. The core installation delivers organized files, backups and a reliable handoff to your editor or content team.
Is the starting price a fixed quote?
No. It is a planning anchor. The final proposal depends on room conditions, number of participants, cameras, set design, storage, construction dependencies and current vendor pricing.
What happens after installation?
We complete an acceptance session, train the team, deliver the SOP and asset documentation, and begin the support period included with your build level.
